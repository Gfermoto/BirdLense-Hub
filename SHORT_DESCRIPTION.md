# BirdLense Hub — short description (EN)

**RU:** [SHORT_DESCRIPTION.ru.md](./SHORT_DESCRIPTION.ru.md)

## Short

Self-hosted bird monitoring for feeders and field setups: YOLO-based detection and species classification, optional BirdNET audio via MQTT, visit timeline, CSV/eBird export, and PWA web UI. Runs in Docker on x86; data stays on your infrastructure.

## Extended

BirdLense Hub combines video and audio cues, a structured species catalog, and visit history for ornithology and citizen science: review uncertain detections, export to eBird or datasets, and integrate with Go2RTC, Frigate, Telegram, and webhooks—without sending core imagery to a vendor cloud.
