## Extended (README tagline, press, catalogues)

**BirdLense Hub** is local-first monitoring for smart bird feeders: computer vision (custom YOLO + tracking) and optional BirdNET audio over MQTT to detect, identify, record, and review visits—timeline, exports, notifications, and integrations (Go2RTC, Frigate, Telegram, webhooks). Runs in Docker on x86; no cloud dependency.
